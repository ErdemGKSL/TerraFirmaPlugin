# HUD element catalogue

Plan item: X-HUD-01 (`plans/90-cross-cutting.md`). Pinned TFC reference: `c3b4d9536` (`docs/porting/tfc-pin.txt`).
Last checked against the plans: 2026-10-19.

## Scope

Every HUD element the plans name on the player's screen outside an inventory GUI: BetterHud elements, the placeholders they read, popups, and the vanilla bars the plans say to hide.

Not listed here (other tracks own them):
- GUI screens and their overlays: M0-GUI-01, M3-GUI-01, M4-ANV-02 (glyph markers), M4-CRUC-01, M4-INGOT-01.
- Item tooltips: M3-HEAT-01 (heat line), M4-MET-01 (heat tooltips), M3-SURV-01 (freshness line), M3-SURV-05 (size line).
- World-space visuals: `entity_renderer` blocks, item displays, pack textures.

Ids are the lower-case plan item id plus an element name (`m3-surv-06-health-bar`). Placeholders use `ph-` (`m1-int-04-ph-health`). Status is `needed` for every row: no element is built in production. Notes say where a spike or a partial check already exists.

## 5. Layout of the bars (remaster 2026-10-22)

Source of truth: `tools/datagen` `HudLayouts.BARS` (placement) and `HudBarArt` (sprites). GUI pixels from the bottom-centre of the screen (`H` = GUI height); the same numbers hold at every GUI scale. Geometry is TFC's `IngameOverlays` at `c3b4d9536`.

| element | sprite | x span | rows | shown |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| health bar + "850/850" | TFC `overlay.png` (0,0)/(0,9), 81x9 | centre-90 .. centre-9 | H-39 .. H-31 | always |
| health text | vanilla font at scale 0.5 (7 px glyphs), white, shadow `FF680000` (TFC `HEALTH_TEXT_SHADOW_COLOR`) | centred on the bar | H-38 .. H-32 (+1 shadow row, still inside the bar) | always |
| hunger bar | TFC food bar (0,19)/(0,24), 81x5, hue turned to orange | centre+11 .. centre+92 | H-35 .. H-31 | always |
| thirst bar | TFC thirst bar (90,19)/(90,24), 81x5 | same | H-41 .. H-37 | always |
| air bar | TFC thirst bar, fill lightened 45 % towards white, 81x5 | same | H-47 .. H-43 | only while `tfp_air < 300` |

Vanilla for comparison: XP bar H-29 .. H-25, hotbar H-22 .. H-1, XP level number centred around H-35 .. H-28 (it falls in the 20 px gap between the columns). So no bar overlaps the XP row or the hotbar, and every right-hand bar has the same height and one 6 px step (TFC's `rightHeight += 6`). The empty state is TFC's dark track with the black outline; a low bar is a short coloured stub at the left of that track.

Divergences from TFC (ledger): hunger is orange, not TFC's green (owner's colour language; green also merged with the green XP bar below it). Air is a bar, not the vanilla bubbles (the bubbles are hidden with the other vanilla rows), with TFC's visibility rule (only below full breath). The demo compass of BetterHud (`compasses/default_compass.yml`, `default: true`) is switched off by the installer: TFC has no HUD compass.

Not verified in game yet: the exact horizontal centring (BetterHud centres a layout on its images; `HudLayouts.TEXT_ON_BAR_DX` = 41 is taken from the old screenshots) and how BetterHud's text shadow (`outline`) renders at scale 0.5.
